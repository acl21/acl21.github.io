Hi! I am a **PhD student** at the [Robot Learning Lab](https://rl.uni-freiburg.de/), supervised by [Prof. Dr. Abhinav Valada](https://rl.uni-freiburg.de/people/valada). My research sits at the intersection of robot learning and world models. I am trying to understand how to build world models that are useful for robot control: how they can be learned from large, diverse data such as robot play and action-free human videos, and how policies, from specialist diffusion policies to generalist VLAs, can be improved inside them.

Before my PhD, I completed my **M.Sc. in Computer Science** at the [University of Freiburg](http://www.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/studies/furtherinformation/concentrationCTS?set_language=en) (2025), where I spent two years as a **Student Research Assistant** at the [Robot Learning Lab](https://rl.uni-freiburg.de/) under [Dr. Iman Nematollahi](https://www.imanema.com/) and [Dr. Tim Welschehold](https://rl.uni-freiburg.de/people/welschehold). My Master's thesis on fine-tuning diffusion policies inside world models grew into [DiWA](https://diwa.cs.uni-freiburg.de/). My Master's project combined geometry-aware motion models with RL for skill chaining ([report](https://akshaychandra.com/assets/pdf/mastersproject-report.pdf), [code](https://github.com/acl21/sac_n_gmm)).

Earlier (2018–2021), I was a **Research Assistant** to [Dr. Vineeth N Balasubramanian](https://www.iith.ac.in/~vineethnb/) at [IIT Hyderabad](https://www.iith.ac.in/), working on deep active learning, and collaborated with [Dr. Wei Guo](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/focus/en/people/k0001_01967.html) ([University of Tokyo](http://park.itc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/Field-Phenomics/ninolab/index.html)) on plant phenotyping.
